## Godot Engine (MIT)

Neko reimplements the Godot Engine in Rust. Layouts, theme formulas, menu
structure and several algorithms (container sorting, the 2D canvas renderer
batching, the SDL joypad driver, the inspector, rulers and zoom widget) are
ported from Godot's source, and `crates/neko-editor/assets/godot_icons` holds
Godot's editor icons. Source: https://github.com/godotengine/godot, tag
`4.7.2-stable`.
